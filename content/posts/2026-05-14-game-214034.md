Nintendo has made a significant move for retro gaming enthusiasts by adding five new titles from the Virtual Boy library to its Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack service. This update brings the total number of available Virtual Boy games to 12 in the West, with players now able to enjoy classics like V-Tetris, Jack Bros., and Space Invaders Virtual Collection. With a total of 16 games planned for release, fans are eagerly anticipating the remaining titles, including Mario Clash and Marios Tennis.

The Virtual Boy, which had a brief and tumultuous life in the 1990s, originally featured only 22 games. This means that once all planned titles are released, players will have access to the majority of the library. However, some games may never make it to the service due to licensing issues, leaving fans wondering which titles will ultimately be available. As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, its exciting to see how Nintendo is embracing its history while catering to a new generation of players.
