The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, a captivating new puzzle game that promises to engage your lateral thinking skills like never before. With its unique format of identifying imposters among a grid of words, Relink challenges players to think outside the box and make connections that arent immediately obvious. This innovative approach to puzzle-solving not only entertains but also sharpens your cognitive abilities.

Each day, players are presented with a fresh puzzle that consists of four rows of four words, where three share a common link while one is an imposter. The thrill lies in uncovering not only the imposters but also the underlying theme that connects them. With tips from the Puzzles Editor, such as starting from the top row and vocalizing the words, players can enhance their chances of cracking the puzzle before their lives run out.
